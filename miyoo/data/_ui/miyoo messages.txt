# Preferences, Settings, Mini Plus.
tip "Sprite smoothing"
	`Smooth (bilinear) sprites look less jagged when they turn. "auto" smooths them whenever the frame has time for it and switches back in heavy scenes, so the frame rate holds.`

tip "D-pad jumps between buttons"
	`In menus and on planets the D-pad jumps the pointer from button to button and row to row. Off: the D-pad always moves a free pointer.`

tip "Button hints"
	`Hold Select or Start for a moment to see what each button does with it, and show a short notice when Select switches between flying and the pointer.`

tip "Rumble"
	`Vibration on hull hits, failing shields, jumps and losing the flagship. Changing this gives a short buzz at the new strength.`

tip "Battery saver"
	`On planets and in menus, draw at most 20 frames a second and let the CPU slow down. Flight is unchanged.`

tip "Start in last save"
	`Skip the main menu when the game starts and go straight to your ship.`

tip "Stay awake in flight"
	`Keep Onion's sleep timer from stopping a long autopilot trip. The console still sleeps on the planet and in menus.`

tip "Save in flight"
	`Start+R2 saves the game wherever you are, and leaving the game in flight (Menu, a flat battery, Onion's sleep) saves first. Off: as on the PC, only landing saves.`

tip "Dim when idle"
	`Dim the screen after this long without a button press; any button brings it back.`
